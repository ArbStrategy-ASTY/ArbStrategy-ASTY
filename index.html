Arbitrage for beginners

Imagine the same sneaker costs $100 in one shop and $110 in another. Buying it for $100 and selling it for $110 creates a $10 difference. That price difference is the basic idea behind arbitrage.

1 Buy cheaper Purchase the same asset where its price is lower.
2 Sell higher Sell it where the available market price is higher.
3 Keep the difference The remaining price difference becomes the arbitrage profit.
Markets move quickly Prices can change at different speeds across exchanges and liquidity pools.
Liquidity differs Pools do not all have the same depth, capital or trading activity.
Prices are not perfectly synchronized Small temporary price gaps create opportunities for arbitrage traders.
