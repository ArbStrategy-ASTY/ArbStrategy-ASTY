The idea

What Rebound does

You choose an asset, a dip level and a take-profit target. Rebound watches the Solana market 24/7. It waits instead of chasing the price, buys only after your configured dip is reached and then watches for the rebound.

Getting started
The basics
What you need before your first strategy.
What do I need to use ASTY Rebound?

You need a connected Phantom wallet, trading capital in USDC, and a small amount of native SOL for Solana network fees.

A new strategy requires at least 25 USDC of strategy capital and your linked Phantom wallet must hold 2,500 ASTY for each active strategy.

Which assets can I trade?

Rebound is built for supported assets on the Solana network. In the current version the strategy selector includes SOL, WBTC, BNB, RAY and ETH.

Trades are routed through Jupiter. Rebound does not hard-code one liquidity pool; it asks Jupiter for an available route for the selected asset.

Why do I need ASTY?

ASTY is the access requirement for creating Rebound strategies. The rule is 2,500 ASTY per active strategy.

Your ASTY is not deposited into Rebound. It stays in your linked Phantom wallet. The balance is checked when a new strategy is created.

What happens if my ASTY balance later drops below the requirement?

Existing strategies are not continuously re-checked against the ASTY gate. The check is performed when you create a new strategy.

If a strategy is paused and you later use Resume, the ASTY gate is not checked again. If you fully stop it and later create a new strategy, the requirement applies again.

Do I need to keep the website open?

No. Once the Rebound Wallet and automated trading permission are set up, active strategies are designed to continue server-side 24/7, even when the website or your phone is closed.

Strategy logic
How a Rebound strategy works
The important terms without the technical noise.
What is the Reference High / High-Water Mark?

The Reference High is the highest market price your strategy has seen while it is waiting for a dip. You may also hear this called the High-Water Mark or HWM.

It can move up when the market makes a new high, but it does not move down while the strategy is waiting.

Example: if your Reference High is $100 and your Dip is 5%, the Buy Trigger is $95. If price first rises to $104, the Reference High becomes $104 and the new 5% Buy Trigger becomes $98.80. This lets the strategy follow a rising market instead of waiting forever for an old fixed price.

When exactly does Rebound buy?

Your Buy Trigger is calculated from:

Reference High × (1 − Dip %)

When the live market price reaches or falls below that trigger, the strategy enters its buy process. Before sending the trade, Rebound checks the market again so it does not blindly chase a price that has already rebounded too far.

What are the strategy presets?
  • Frequent: Dip 3% · Take Profit 2.5%
  • Balanced: Dip 5% · Take Profit 4%
  • Deep Dip: Dip 8% · Take Profit 6%
  • Custom: choose your own supported Dip and Take Profit values.

Presets only define the strategy levels. They do not guarantee that a trade will occur or that a cycle will be profitable.

Why is Take Profit based on the actual buy fill?

The trigger price is only the point at which Rebound starts the buy process. Your actual swap can execute slightly above or below it.

That is why the Take Profit target is calculated from the real average buy fill. This keeps the target connected to the price you actually paid.

What does “Position open” mean?

It means the strategy has already used its reserved USDC to buy the selected asset. Rebound is now watching the live price for the Take Profit target or, if enabled, the Stop Loss.

What does Stop Loss do?

Stop Loss is optional and is off by default. If enabled and the configured Stop Loss is reached, Rebound attempts to sell the open asset position back to USDC.

After a Stop Loss exit, the strategy becomes Paused. Its remaining USDC stays reserved for that strategy until you choose Resume or fully stop the strategy.

What happens when I Resume a paused strategy?

The same strategy starts again with the same settings and reserved capital. Rebound sets a fresh Reference High from the current market price.

The ASTY requirement is not checked again for this Resume action.

What is Auto Repeat?

With Auto Repeat enabled, a successful Take Profit does not end the strategy. Rebound automatically starts another cycle and begins watching the market for a new dip.

What is the difference between Fixed Capital and Compound?

With Compound off, the next cycle uses the strategy's original capital again and realized profit becomes free USDC.

Example: 100 USDC becomes 103.40 USDC. The next cycle still uses 100 USDC and 3.40 USDC becomes available.

With Compound on, the full 103.40 USDC becomes the capital for the next cycle. The Compound setting is fixed after the strategy is created.

What is Cycle History?

Each strategy keeps its own completed-cycle history. It can show the buy fill, sell fill, capital used, capital returned, realized result and links to the on-chain buy and sell transactions.

Wallet & security
Where your funds are and who can do what
Important questions before you fund an automated wallet.
What is the Rebound Wallet?

The Rebound Wallet is a dedicated Solana wallet used by ASTY Rebound for your trading capital, network-fee SOL and open strategy assets.

It is separate from your Phantom wallet. You can see and copy the Rebound Wallet address directly in the Rebound interface.

Where is my Rebound Wallet?

The wallet is created as an embedded Solana wallet through Privy and is permanently linked to your Rebound account and linked Phantom wallet.

The funds themselves are on the Solana blockchain at the Rebound Wallet address. The wallet remains the same between sessions.

Do I get a seed phrase or passphrase for the Rebound Wallet?

ASTY Rebound does not ask you to enter or reveal your Phantom recovery phrase, and the normal Rebound flow does not rely on you managing a traditional seed phrase for the embedded Rebound Wallet.

Never enter your Phantom seed phrase or recovery phrase into ASTY Rebound, a chat, or any website claiming it is required for Rebound.

Who can access the Rebound Wallet?

Your Rebound Wallet is used by the automated trading system through a restricted Privy trading authorization. This is what allows a strategy to execute while your browser is closed.

The automation is designed for policy-protected trading actions rather than unrestricted wallet use. This is different from keeping funds only in Phantom: funds allocated to Rebound are placed in a separate wallet specifically so automated execution can take place.

Can Rebound withdraw my funds to any wallet?

The Rebound withdrawal flow is restricted to your permanently linked Phantom wallet. There is no free-form withdrawal address field.

A withdrawal requires Phantom approval, after which the Rebound Wallet adds its required signature and the transaction is broadcast to Solana.

Why does automated trading not ask Phantom for every swap?

That is the purpose of the Rebound Wallet. When automated trading is enabled, a restricted server-side signer can execute supported strategy trades according to the configured trading policy.

If Phantom confirmation were required for every buy and sell, Rebound could not react to the market 24/7 while you were asleep or offline.

Is the Rebound Wallet the same as my Phantom wallet?

No. Phantom is your linked user wallet. The Rebound Wallet is the dedicated execution wallet.

Your Phantom wallet is used for identity, the ASTY holding requirement, deposits and withdrawal approval. Strategy funds are moved into the Rebound Wallet before they can be used by Rebound.

Can I connect a different Phantom wallet later?

Rebound is designed around a permanent mapping between the linked Phantom identity and its Rebound Wallet. It is not intended to behave like a freely switchable trading wallet.

Funds & withdrawals
How capital is separated
Available funds, allocated funds and gas all have different jobs.
What is Available USDC?

Available USDC is the part of your Rebound Wallet balance that is not reserved by a strategy. It can be used to create another strategy or withdrawn back to your linked Phantom wallet.

What is Allocated / Strategy Capital?

This is capital reserved for active strategies. Each strategy owns its own reserved amount.

Example: with 100 USDC in the Rebound Wallet, a 50 USDC strategy and a 25 USDC strategy leave 25 USDC available. One strategy cannot simply spend capital reserved for another strategy.

Why do I need SOL if my strategy uses USDC?

Native SOL is used for Solana network fees. It is separate from your strategy capital.

Rebound recommends about 0.02 SOL as a comfortable gas reserve. While strategies are active, the minimum protected reserve is 0.005 SOL.

Can I withdraw USDC that is reserved by a strategy?

No. Only free USDC can be withdrawn. Strategy capital stays reserved until the strategy releases it according to its state.

What happens when I stop a strategy?

If the strategy is still only watching the market, stopping it releases its reserved USDC back to your Available Balance.

If a position is already open, Stop & close position first attempts to sell the trading asset back to USDC and then stop the strategy.

Why can't I withdraw all my SOL while strategies are active?

Rebound keeps a minimum native SOL reserve so active strategies are not left without enough gas for future on-chain transactions. The protected minimum is 0.005 SOL.

Trading & risk
What happens on-chain
Automation reduces manual work. It does not remove market risk.
How does Rebound find a trade route?

Rebound uses Jupiter to request a route for the selected Solana asset. It is not tied to one hard-coded pool.

Before automated execution, the system performs route and transaction safety checks and simulates the transaction.

What does 0.5% slippage mean?

The configured 0.5% slippage is a maximum tolerance for execution — it does not mean every trade automatically loses 0.5%.

The final fill can still differ from the displayed market price because markets move, liquidity changes and swaps can include pool or route costs.

Can Rebound guarantee a profit?

No. Rebound automates a strategy; it does not remove trading risk. Markets can continue falling after a buy, liquidity can change, and an exit can execute at a different price from the displayed target.

Stop Loss can limit the strategy according to your configured level, but it also cannot guarantee an exact exit price in every market condition.

What happens if Jupiter cannot find a safe route?

Rebound should not blindly send a mismatched trade. The execution process checks the requested asset, amount and route before execution. If the required checks fail, the trade is not supposed to proceed as a normal successful execution.

Why can the displayed price differ slightly from my actual fill?

The displayed market price is a reference price. A real swap happens on-chain through available liquidity, so the average executed price can differ.

For that reason Rebound stores the actual confirmed fill and uses that fill as the basis for the strategy's Take Profit calculation.

What if the market reaches my Dip and immediately rebounds?

Rebound performs a fresh price check before a triggered buy is sent. If the market has already moved back above the allowed trigger range, the strategy can return to watching instead of blindly buying after the opportunity has passed.

Important: ASTY Rebound executes real crypto trades. Prices can move quickly and losses are possible. Only allocate capital you are comfortable exposing to market and smart-contract / infrastructure risk.
