Arbitrage for beginners

Imagine the same sneaker costs $100 in one shop and $110 in another. Buying it for $100 and selling it for $110 creates a $10 difference. That price difference is the basic idea behind arbitrage.

1 Buy cheaper Purchase the same asset where its price is lower.
2 Sell higher Sell it where the available market price is higher.
3 Keep the difference The remaining price difference becomes the arbitrage profit.
Markets move quickly Prices can change at different speeds across exchanges and liquidity pools.
Liquidity differs Pools do not all have the same depth, capital or trading activity.
Prices are not perfectly synchronized Small temporary price gaps create opportunities for arbitrage traders.

How ArbStrategy uses market activity

ArbStrategy does not allocate capital randomly. It provides liquidity to selected ASTY pools and measures their real performance. Trading and arbitrage activity within those pools can generate LP income.

Measure performance Pool activity, revenue and capital efficiency are evaluated using measurable data.
Strengthen efficient pools Pools that use capital efficiently can receive additional liquidity.
Limit weak allocation Pools with weak efficiency are not automatically expanded.
Capital follows results Allocation decisions are based on measurable performance rather than emotion or speculation.

Performance-based capital allocation

The purpose is not to add more liquidity everywhere. The objective is to deploy capital where measurable activity and income indicate that additional liquidity can strengthen the system efficiently.

Relative benchmarks Pools are compared with the strongest current performers rather than evaluated in isolation.
Efficiency tiers Measured performance helps categorize pools and prioritize future allocation.
Controlled growth Additional capital is limited when pool efficiency declines as liquidity increases.
Community participation Major proposals are prepared by the core team and can be presented to the community for voting via Telegram.
